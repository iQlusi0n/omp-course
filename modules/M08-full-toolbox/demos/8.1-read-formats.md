# Demo 8.1 — `read` beyond text (~60 s)

Captured on `omp/18.3.1` with `omp read <path>` (the shell twin of the `read` tool — identical output, no model). Fixtures were a throwaway copy of the lab layout (`data/lab.sqlite` with `users`/`orders`, `fixtures/bundle.zip`, `docs/spec.pdf`, `docs/analysis.ipynb`); row counts in your lab will differ.
