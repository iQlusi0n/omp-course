What to point at when a learner asks "where did it hand off?":

1. The `todo` card — before it, no `edit` would have switched anything.
2. The first `edit` card that completed **after** the todo — the switch happens right after it, hence the notice sits between the first and second edit.
3. The model chip (`⬢ …` in the status line) — different model name before and after that notice.
