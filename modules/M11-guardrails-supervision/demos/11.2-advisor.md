Why the note restarted the agent: the primary had already produced a **terminal answer with no
queued work**. In that state a `concern` is preserved as a visible card and only re-enters
context on your next message (`#4840`); a `blocker` is the exception — it triggers a turn because
it means broken or unverified work was handed off (`#5628`). `WATCHDOG.md` told the advisor that
a swallowed failure the caller relies on is a `blocker`, so the agent resumed on its own. After
this delivery, `advisor.immuneTurns` (default 3) routes further concerns/blockers as asides for
three completed turns.
