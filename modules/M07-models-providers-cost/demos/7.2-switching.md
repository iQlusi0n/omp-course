$ omp models --json | python3 -c 'import json,sys; d=json.load(sys.stdin); print([(m["provider"], m["id"], m["selector"]) for m in d["models"]])'
[('mock', 'mock-1', 'mock/mock-1'), ('mock-backup', 'mock-1', 'mock-backup/mock-1')]

`--json` objects carry `provider`, `id`, `selector`, `name`, `contextWindow`, `maxTokens`, `cost`, `input`, `kind`, `reasoning`, `thinking`.

The status line shows the live model as a chip (`⬢ <display name>`); it is what `Ctrl+P` / `Alt+P` change:

```
 π > ⬢ Mock 1 (omp-course-lab) > 🗑 m07-smoke/elsewhere ▶───────────19%─╎──────────────┃─────────────────────────────33K─
                                                                                            ^ context used   ^ window
```

After a fallback (7.5) or `Alt+P` the same chip reads the new model, e.g. `⬢ Mock 1 (backup)`.
